KuroCat

A native little cat that sits on your keyboard.

Type and it taps the keys with you. Shake the mouse and its eyes go wide. KuroCat runs natively on macOS, Windows, Linux and UOS, and every keystroke stays in local memory to drive the animation. Nothing is logged or uploaded.

Whatever key you hit, it hits too.

It keeps up with coding, typing and held WASD in games. Hold 3 keys at once and it reaches out a back foot; hold 4 and the tail joins in. On a graphics tablet it switches to a pen grip by itself.

macOS IOHIDManager · Windows Raw Input · Linux XInput2 / evdev · full NKRO

Flick the mouse and it starts hunting.

Head, ears and whiskers follow your cursor with a 2.5D spherical turn. Shake the mouse fast and it crouches low, its pupils swell into big round hunting eyes, and the tail flicks faster.

Try shaking your mouse fast on this screen

Pet its head, or lift it by the scruff.

Stroke its head and it squints, folds its ears and sparkles. Press and drag to lift it by the scruff: the body stretches into a long cat while paws and tail swing with 2D inertia.

New in v1.4.4: front-facing long-cat drag, and dragging can be turned off

Ignore it and it curls up to sleep.

Left alone for a while, it yawns, curls into a ball and puffs out a few z’s. Once asleep it drops its frame rate to save power.

Adaptive 30 / 20 / 10 fps, with an optional 60 fps mode

When you go live, it is your co-host.

Turn on OBS transparent window capture or the #00FF00 green-screen mode with one click and add it to OBS, Douyin Live Companion or Bilibili Live. Fullscreen auto-hide pauses while you stream, so the cat never vanishes mid-show.

Under 1 MB, native on four systems.

No Electron, no bundled browser. Each platform is written against its own graphics and input APIs, and the cat sits quietly in your menu bar or tray.

  • macOS

    Swift · CoreGraphics · IOHIDManager

    One build for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. Lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.

  • Windows

    C++17 · GDI+ · Raw Input

    Windows 7 SP1 through 11. A single portable exe with no runtime dependencies, living in the tray.

  • Linux

    C++17 · GTK3 / Cairo · XInput2 / evdev

    Ubuntu, Debian, Deepin, Arch, Fedora. Double-click on X11; Wayland supported too.

  • UOS / Kylin

    x86_64 · ARM64

    UnionTech UOS 20 and Kylin V10, on Phytium and Kunpeng too. The tar.gz installs without sudo.

Ask on GitHub
Does KuroCat record or upload what I type?

No. Keyboard and mouse signals stay in local memory and only drive the animation and counters. Nothing is logged and no keystrokes are uploaded. The only network request is the update check against the official download site, and the full source is MIT licensed for anyone to audit.

How is KuroCat different from classic Bongo Cat?

KuroCat is written natively in Swift, Win32 C++ and GTK3 Cairo, about 850 KB as a single Windows exe, and runs on macOS, Windows, Linux and UOS. Beyond NKRO key tapping it adds 2.5D head tracking, hunting eyes, a graphics-tablet pen mode, OBS transparent and green-screen modes, scruff dragging with inertia, and an online Skin Workshop.

How do I add KuroCat to OBS, Douyin Live Companion or Bilibili Live?

Right-click the cat or the tray icon, open Settings and turn on OBS Transparent Window Capture or OBS Green Screen (#00FF00), then add a window capture source in your streaming app. Fullscreen auto-hide pauses automatically in OBS mode, so the cat never disappears.

How do I open Settings after turning on full click-through?

Transparent areas already let clicks through. With full click-through on, click the KuroCat icon in the macOS menu bar or the Windows / Linux tray. You can also hold Option on macOS or press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + P on Windows.

macOS says it can’t open the app, or typing does nothing?

On first launch, right-click KuroCat.app, choose Open and confirm. Then enable KuroCat in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring and restart it from the menu bar.

Is the Windows SmartScreen warning safe to ignore?

New releases of indie open-source apps often trigger SmartScreen as an uncommon download. Check that the SHA-256 on this page matches the GitHub release, then choose More info → Run anyway, or build it yourself from source.

Does it run on UnionTech UOS and Kylin?

Yes. Linux builds target Debian 10 (glibc 2.28), which covers UOS 20 and Kylin V10, on both x86_64 and ARM64. If you can’t enter an admin password, use the no-sudo tar.gz installer.

How do I turn my own cat into a skin?

Open the KuroCat Skin Workshop, upload a photo to sample its coat colors, draw pattern regions with the pen tool and add accessory stickers. Drag the exported .kurocat file onto your desktop cat, or submit it to the community plaza.
